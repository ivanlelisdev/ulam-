# Product vision

Ulam? helps a household answer “Anong ulam?” and carry that decision from the weekly plan to the market and the stove. It serves Filipino home cooks first, including budget-conscious shoppers, families sharing meal decisions, and people cooking or shopping with unreliable connectivity. Filipino dishes and familiar ingredient names should be easy to discover, with regional names and origins treated carefully.

## Jobs to be done

- **Decide** · Find a realistic dish for tonight or fill open slots for the week using available time, ingredients and household preferences.
- **Prepare** · Scale servings, see what the week needs and check off groceries at the market.
- **Cook** · Reopen the recipe in the kitchen and follow one clear step at a time.
- **Reuse** · Save a successful rotation as a template and keep family variations in locally owned recipes.

## Experience principles

Begin with useful content before asking for settings. Make Filipino and English ingredient aliases searchable. Explain suggestions in plain language, for example “25 min · uses chicken.” Keep accept, skip and undo actions within reach. Design for phone screens, interrupted sessions and one-handed use.

## Validation

In moderated sessions, ask a new user to find a dish, plan three dinners, generate groceries and follow a cooking step after going offline. Record time, mistaken taps, completion and reported confusion. Ask a returning user to reuse a week and adjust servings. Suggested initial usability target is four of five participants completing the first flow unaided. Collect results outside the app during testing.
